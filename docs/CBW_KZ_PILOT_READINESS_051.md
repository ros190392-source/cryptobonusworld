## 1. Decision

Kazakhstan is selected as the first provisional Portal Factory pilot because the repository already contains the broadest usable combination of:

- country-level GEO evidence;
- one fully closed and merged deep-research lifecycle for Binance;
- one substantial but conflicting OKX research package;
- existing Bybit country evidence in the current GEO model;
- payment/P2P and offer-boundary research topics;
- English and Russian presentation demand.

This decision does **not** authorize a country ranking, production country pages, affiliate activation, indexability or deployment.

## 3. Pilot publication gates

Kazakhstan cannot become public until all of the following are true:

1. At least three exchange market profiles validate under the Portal Factory contracts.
2. Supporting source packets include current source dates, capture references and digests.
3. Material contradictions are resolved or remain visibly under review.
4. Country availability is separated from local offer eligibility.
5. Payment/P2P statements identify direction, currency, eligibility and freshness limitations.
6. A ranking methodology version is frozen.
7. A non-empty ranking snapshot is explicitly approved by the owner.
8. English content package is approved.
9. Russian locale rendering passes terminology and fact-parity QA.
10. Canonical, hreflang, sitemap, schema, browser and affiliate gates pass.
11. Indexability and production publication receive separate authorization.
