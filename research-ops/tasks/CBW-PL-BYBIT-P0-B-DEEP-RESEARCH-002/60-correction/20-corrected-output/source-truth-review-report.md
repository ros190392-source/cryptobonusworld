## Executive finding

Bybit EU has a materially stronger Poland evidence posture than a merely reachable global exchange:

1. Austria's FMA granted Bybit EU GmbH a MiCAR CASP authorisation under Article 63, with a defined service scope.
2. Bybit EU says it operates a dedicated European platform for eligible EEA residents and that authorised services may be passported where applicable requirements are satisfied and services are actually made available.
3. Current Poland-facing evidence includes PLN deposits via BLIK and Poland-focused PLN trading pairs.

Those facts support a **candidate `AVAILABLE_WITH_LIMITS` posture for core regulated services**, not blanket availability for every Bybit product.

## MiCAR authorisation and service scope

FMA's authorisation record identifies Bybit EU GmbH, FN 636180i, and lists custody/administration, exchange crypto-assets for funds, exchange crypto-assets for other crypto-assets, placing, and transfer services. Bybit EU's own June 2026 MiCAR materials describe the dedicated EEA platform and emphasize that product/service availability can vary by country.

The research therefore separates:
- legal authorisation of the entity and service class;
- actual availability of a service in Poland;
- account/KYC eligibility;
- optional or unregulated products.

## Poland-facing fiat and spot evidence

Current Bybit EU help documentation lists PLN with BLIK for fiat deposits and requires Identity Verification. A May 2026 Poland-focused announcement lists USDC/PLN, BTC/PLN, ETH/PLN and SOL/PLN and specifically addresses users in Poland.

This is sufficient to record current Poland support for PLN deposits/BLIK and the named PLN pairs, with normal user/account/regulatory caveats.

## KYC

Bybit EU requires EEA users to complete Standard Verification. Current KYC documentation also ties account/product functionality to verification state. KYC is therefore a mandatory account requirement, but it is not evidence that every optional product is available in Poland.

## Fiat withdrawal

Bybit EU offers fiat withdrawal for selected currencies and methods, but the captured current FAQ does not enumerate PLN. Exact PLN withdrawal remains **NOT VERIFIED** in this record.

## Earn

Bybit EU documents Earn products and explicitly states that Bybit Earn is not regulated under MiCAR. Product eligibility is separately jurisdiction-gated. This record does not bind a direct positive Poland eligibility statement for Earn, so Poland Earn remains **UNDER_REVIEW**.

## Bybit Card

An EEA card application guide and a Polish-language Card surface are public. The application guide is country/eligibility gated and the captured public evidence does not conclusively bind final issuance eligibility for Poland. Card therefore remains **UNDER_REVIEW** rather than `available`.

## Derivatives

No current Poland-specific positive Bybit EU derivatives/futures evidence was captured for this run. The FMA MiCAR service list is not automatically authority for derivatives that may fall under other regulatory frameworks. Derivatives remain **NOT VERIFIED**.

## Promotions and referral eligibility

Bybit EU exposes a current Poland offers hub and has run Poland-specific campaigns. A referral marathon included Poland through 31 July 2026. That historical campaign proves Poland-targeted referral marketing existed, but not a current campaign.

CryptoBonusWorld's owner-confirmed global Bybit destination/code (`partner.bybit.com` / `CRYPTOBONUSW`) is a separate commercial authority. This research does not prove that identity is accepted by or eligible for the current Bybit EU Poland entity/campaign. Country referral/bonus eligibility remains **UNDER_REVIEW**.
